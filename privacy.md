# Ninja Rise - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Ninja Rise (the "Game"), published on Google Play by LIVA GAMES, handles information. A Turkish version is below.

## Information stored on your device

Your game progress (score, coins, owned items and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, sell or share this data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction data and diagnostic information. Users in the EU/EEA and the UK are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. For details see https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

## In-app purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads and premium items are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

## Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at avufukacikalin@gmail.com
